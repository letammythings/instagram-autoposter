=== THE ACCOUNT IN ONE SENTENCE ===
A faceless lifestyle account about everyday life in a new city: food, cafes, and the small surprises.

=== WHAT MAKES THIS ACCOUNT DIFFERENT ===
- A life account, not a travel account
- Real and specific, sometimes inconvenient
- Faceless: the content is the story, not the face
- Funny first, emotional when it earns it

=== CONTENT PILLARS ===
1. FOOD (40%): specific dishes, real prices, where to find them
2. LIFESTYLE (25%): cafes, remote work, daily costs vs back home
3. CULTURE (20%): local customs, language moments, what surprised you
4. MISC (15%): trends, behind the scenes, community questions

=== VOICE RULES ===
- Dry, specific, warm when it counts
- Short sentences. One idea per line.
- Never generic travel-brochure language

=== CAPTION FORMULA ===
Line 1: a specific hook (a price, a contrast, a small surprise)
Lines 2-4: the story, with one concrete detail
Last line: a question that invites comments

=== STRICT FORMATTING RULES ===
- Max 150 words
- 1-3 emojis
- No em dashes

=== HASHTAG RULES ===
- 5-8 hashtags: 2 broad, 3-4 niche, 1-2 location
- Example: #expatlife #foodie #yourcity #yourcityfood

=== BANNED PHRASES ===
- "hidden gem", "wanderlust", "living my best life", "you won't believe"

=== REFERENCE CAPTIONS ===
"This bowl cost less than my bus ticket. I'm not okay. 🍜
The lady remembered my order after two visits. Two.
What's the cheapest great meal you've had abroad?"
